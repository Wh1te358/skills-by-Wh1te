# Force Method Review Sheet

For a one-degree indeterminate structure, remove one redundant to form the basic system. Compute the released displacement caused by external loading and the flexibility coefficient caused by a unit redundant.

The compatibility equation is `Delta_1P + delta_11 X_1 = 0` when the released support displacement is zero. Solve for `X_1`, restore the redundant, and superpose the basic-system and redundant-force internal forces.

Verify the result by substituting `X_1` into the compatibility equation and by checking equilibrium after superposition.
